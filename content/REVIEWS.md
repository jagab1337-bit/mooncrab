## Managing guest reviews

Open **Admin → Guest Reviews**. Add the guest’s original text, an authorized image, or both. A display name, review date and original rating are optional. Use an approved name or leave it blank. Internal notes and uploaded originals are private.

Use the image redaction tool to cover private details before publishing. Check the full preview for details that a cropped thumbnail might conceal. Use the thumbnail position controls to choose the crop. JPG, PNG and WebP uploads can be up to 2 MiB and 20 megapixels. The homepage uses a resized thumbnail and a separate full-resolution, uncropped WebP with metadata stripped. Original files remain private and can be downloaded from the editor, including earlier attachments after a replacement.

**Publish Review** makes the review available on the homepage immediately after saving. An open homepage refreshes when it regains focus or within a minute while visible. Featured reviews appear first, with manual order applied within each group. Unpublish to hide a review, or archive it and restore it to draft later. The existing Airbnb review is included in the library and can be managed like other reviews.
