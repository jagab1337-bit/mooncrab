# Mooncrab brand assets

The site follows the supplied `/mooncrab` Dropbox palette and actual property photographs. The compact header uses the original crescent/crab symbol extracted from the owner-supplied house-rules PDF, paired with a matching text wordmark. The brown logo panel, hero seal, and footer logo were removed. The original board is retained for provenance; it is no longer rendered in the public layout.

Palette: cream #F7EEE3, sand #B7B09E, sage #839689, ochre #DEB52D, cocoa #472F29. Darker supporting text colors preserve legibility on cream.

The guide specifies Tropikal, Lekton, and Arial Nova. No font files were supplied. Lekton is self-hosted under its packaged license; the display uses Cormorant Garamond until a licensed Tropikal webfont is provided, and body copy uses Arial with system fallbacks.
